Privacy · What is stored and sent

What this app keeps, and what it sends

On this computer

  • thecurve.terminal.settings: your settings. Text size, contrast, movement, background, shooting stars, the opening animation, and whether Discord status is on.
  • thecurve.terminal.seen: the last version whose What's new you opened, so the app can mark the link when there is something new.
  • Both live in the app's own storage, in your Windows profile at %LOCALAPPDATA%\in.thecurve.terminal. Microsoft Edge WebView2, which draws the window, keeps its own cache in the same folder.
  • When the journal arrives, its entries will be stored on this computer too. This page will say exactly where before that update ships.

In Windows

  • If "Open when Windows starts" is on: one value named TheCurveTerminal under HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\Microsoft\Windows\CurrentVersion\Run, pointing at the app. Switching it off removes it.
  • Setup records the app under HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\Microsoft\Windows\CurrentVersion\Uninstall\TheCurveTerminal, so it appears in Installed apps and can be removed from there.

What leaves this computer

  • Discord status, while it is on. It is on by default. The app talks only to the Discord app already running on this computer. Discord then shows on your profile: "The Curve Terminal", one short line from a fixed list of twenty (for example "Reading an old trade"), how long the app has been open, and a button that links to thecurve.in. Anyone who can see your Discord activity sees this. Discord's own activity privacy settings apply.
  • The status never includes your trades, notes, settings or anything you write. The lines are chosen at random and do not describe what you are doing.
  • To stop it, switch off "Show activity on Discord" in Settings. The status clears straight away.
  • Nothing else. No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no accounts and no update checks. The app makes no network requests of its own.
  • The links to thecurve.in and the Discord community open in your browser, and only when you press them.

Removing it

  • Uninstalling from Installed apps removes the app, its shortcuts and its startup entry. Tick "Also delete my settings" to remove its storage folder as well.

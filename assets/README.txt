THE CURVE TERMINAL
by s7ven

A trading journal from The Curve (https://thecurve.in).
Version 0.6.0, 12 October 2026.


WHAT IT IS

A quiet place to write down what you saw in the market, and read it back
later. New entry records a trade; Overview lists them all; Stats works
out forty figures from them, each with its sample size and where it
misleads; the Calendar shows each day; Accounts keeps your prop rules,
fees and payouts. Charts of your trades come in a later update.

Replay plays your own exported bars forward on a candlestick chart, so
you can practise market, limit and stop orders with bracket presets.
Every replay trade is practice, kept apart from the journal.

The Folio keeps research pages: headings, colour, highlights, lists and
screenshots. Mark the lines that matter as important (shown or hidden)
and the Digest gathers them from every page.

Atlas shows the market's background from official public data:
Treasury yields and auctions, the New York Fed's overnight rates, the
CFTC's Commitments of Traders, EIA's weekly petroleum stocks, the ECB's
euro reference rates, a calendar of scheduled releases, and the Federal
Reserve Board's press releases. Each figure names its publisher and date.
Its first page, How it connects, explains how each part is linked to NQ,
how that link is usually read and where the read goes wrong. It does not
say what NQ will do.

It is an instrument for looking, not a machine for deciding. It offers no
strategies, no signals and no one telling you what to buy.


WHERE THINGS ARE

The app        The folder you chose in setup, usually
               %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\The Curve Terminal
Your journal   %LOCALAPPDATA%\in.thecurve.terminal\journal.json
               (with the version before your last change beside it,
               as journal.bak.json; copy it somewhere safe as a backup)
Your settings  %LOCALAPPDATA%\in.thecurve.terminal
               (kept on this computer only)
Your Folio     %LOCALAPPDATA%\in.thecurve.terminal\folio.json
               (with folio.bak.json, and screenshots in folio-images)
Replay files   %LOCALAPPDATA%\in.thecurve.terminal\replay
               (a compact copy of each file of bars you load, and
               the practice log)
Atlas data     %LOCALAPPDATA%\in.thecurve.terminal\backdrop
               (the last copy of each public source, for offline use)


WHAT LEAVES THIS COMPUTER

Discord status, while it is on. It is on by default. The app tells the
Discord app on this computer to show "The Curve Terminal", a short line
from a fixed list (for example "Reading an old trade"), how long the app
has been open, and a link to thecurve.in. It never includes your trades or
anything you write. Switch it off in Settings, under Discord. Your journal
is never sent anywhere.

Atlas's requests for public data, while fetching is on. It is on
by default. While one of its pages is open, the app fetches published
figures from the US Treasury, the Federal Reserve Bank of New York, the
Federal Reserve Board, the CFTC, the European Central Bank and the US
Energy Information Administration. The requests name the app and its
version and carry nothing about you. Switch it off in Settings, under
Atlas. Sources, in the app, lists every address.

Nothing else. No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no accounts and
no update checks.

REMOVING IT

Open Settings in Windows, then Apps, then Installed apps, find
The Curve Terminal and choose Uninstall. Or run uninstall.exe in the
app's folder.
